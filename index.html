Project: competitor landscape

A work sample from an internship task: comparing how competing photography services price and package their offers for international visitors in two Vietnamese cities, and which services customers mention in public reviews.

Confidentiality: company, client and competitor details are masked or partially hidden to protect data. Figures are rounded and shown for illustration.

What the data shows: some competitors charge a flat fee whatever the group size, others scale per person (up to roughly 4x for four guests). Pose guidance and costume rental come up most in reviews, so customers value help on the day, not only the photos.
